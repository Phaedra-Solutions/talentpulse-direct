## 3. Categories of Personal Data We Collect

### 3.1 Candidate Profile & Recruitment Data
When you apply to join the TalentPulse candidate pool, we collect:
*   **Identity & Contact Data:** Legal name, email address, international phone number, country, and residential city.
*   **Professional & Technical Credentials:** Primary engineering track, years of experience, primary and secondary technology proficiencies, GitHub profile URL, LinkedIn profile URL, personal portfolio URLs, and previous employment history.
*   **Work Preferences & Logistics:** Availability status (immediate, 2 weeks, 1 month), target hourly rate (USD), target annual compensation (USD), home timezone, and guaranteed US/European business hour working overlap (hours per day).
*   **Pitch & Media Submissions:** Written personal pitch, uploaded resume files (`.pdf`, `.docx`), and voluntary video pitch recordings (Loom or YouTube video URLs).
*   **Recruiter Assessments:** Internal evaluation notes, stage transitions, and AI-assisted screening summaries generated during our 4-stage evaluation process.

### 3.2 Client & Business Procurement Data
When you submit an enterprise hire request, we collect:
*   **Client Contact Information:** Client representative name, corporate email address, corporate telephone number, and country of operation.
*   **Enterprise Information:** Company / Business name, industry, and organizational website.
*   **Staffing & Project Specifications:** Required engineering roles, technology stack requirements, target budget ranges, expected start timeline, and project description.
*   **Negotiation & Outreach History:** Recruiter offline call logs, mutual non-disclosure agreements (NDAs), and interview scheduling records.

### 3.3 Technical & Telemetry Data
When you browse our website, our servers automatically collect:
*   **Device & Connection Information:** IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device identifiers, and language preferences.
*   **Usage Telemetry:** Pages viewed, timestamps, referring URLs, interaction durations, and session events.
*   **Authentication Data:** Cryptographic session tokens, password hashes (salted PBKDF2 with SHA-512), and audit log timestamps.

This section applies exclusively to California residents under the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018 as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020.

### 5.2 We Do NOT Sell or Share Personal Information
TalentPulse Direct **does not sell** your personal information for monetary consideration, and **does not share** your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not sell or share the personal information of minors under 16 years of age.

### 5.3 Your California Privacy Rights
California residents have the following enforceable rights:
*   **Right to Know & Access:** You may request details on the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected about you over the preceding 12 months.
*   **Right to Delete:** You may request deletion of personal information we have collected from you, subject to legal exceptions (such as tax compliance or active contractual obligations).
*   **Right to Correct:** You may request correction of inaccurate personal information maintained in your profile.
*   **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We will never deny services, charge different rates, or degrade quality if you exercise your statutory privacy rights.
*   **Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information:** We strictly limit processing of sensitive personal information to that necessary to deliver the recruitment services requested.

To exercise your California privacy rights, email `privacy@talentpulse.dev` with the subject line *"California Privacy Request"*.

## 9. Data Retention & Erasure Schedule

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Policy:
*   **Candidate Applications:** Retained for the duration of the candidate's active enrollment in our talent pool. Profiles inactive for 24 consecutive months are automatically archived or erased.
*   **Client Hire Requests:** Retained for 3 years following submission to maintain commercial records, audit logs, and tax compliance.
*   **Administrator & Audit Logs:** Stored for 12 months for forensic and cybersecurity compliance, after which they are permanently purged.
*   **Right to Erasure Requests:** When an applicant requests deletion, their profile, internal notes, and resume references are permanently deleted within 30 days.

## 10. Information Security & Technical Measures

TalentPulse employs state-of-the-art security controls:
1.  **Cryptographic Password Security:** Administrator credentials are salted with 16 cryptographically random bytes and hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-512 over 100,000 iterations. Plaintext passwords are never logged or stored.
2.  **Session Security:** Sessions use HMAC-SHA256 cryptographic signatures stored in HTTP-Only, SameSite=None, Secure cookies with 24-hour expiration.
3.  **Database Row-Level Security (RLS):** Supabase PostgreSQL database tables enforce strict RLS policies preventing unauthorized reads or cross-tenant data leaks.
4.  **Transport Security:** Strict Transport Security (HSTS) and TLS 1.3 encryption on all public endpoints.

## 11. Your Data Subject Rights (DSR) & How to Exercise Them

Under applicable global data protection laws, you possess the following rights:
*   **Access & Portability:** Request a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used machine-readable format (`.json` or `.csv`).
*   **Rectification:** Correct inaccurate or incomplete profile records.
*   **Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request permanent deletion of your profile and submitted media.
*   **Restriction of Processing:** Request temporary suspension of candidate profile matching.
*   **Objection to Processing:** Object to processing based on legitimate business interests.
*   **Withdrawal of Consent:** Withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

*   **Subject Line:** `Privacy Data Subject Request — [Your Full Name]`
*   **Verification:** To protect your security, we will verify your identity via email confirmation before processing substantive requests. We respond to all verified requests within **30 calendar days** at no cost.
